Inferred (not asked): static HTML/CSS/vanilla JS, no build step, so the site can be hosted on any static host (GitHub Pages, Netlify, Cloudflare Pages) and edited by hand.

## Users

Men in and around Mannheim who need a suit: for work, for an occasion, and above all for their own wedding (groom, best man, family). They find the shop through Google Maps, Instagram and TikTok, usually on a phone, and want to know three things fast: what the shop is like, whether the advice is good, and how to get there or call.

## Product Purpose

PARLA Herrenmode is a menswear store (Herrenmodengeschäft) in Mannheim's city centre. The website replaces the missing "Website" entry on the Google Maps listing and turns social-media and Maps visitors into store visits and phone calls. Success means a visitor calls, plans a visit, or saves the address.

## Positioning

Personal, in-store advice that gets a man into the right suit quickly. Customers describe walking in, saying what they need, and leaving with their "Traum-Anzug" in a very short time. Focus areas confirmed by the user: suits (Anzüge) and wedding suits (Hochzeitsanzüge).

## Product Principles

1. The store is the product: every page path ends at the door or the phone.
2. Advice over inventory: sell the experience of being dressed well, not a catalogue.
3. The wedding is the flagship occasion and gets its own clear path.
4. Real proof only: Google rating, real review words, real address.
5. Phone-first: most visitors arrive from Maps, Instagram or TikTok on a phone.
